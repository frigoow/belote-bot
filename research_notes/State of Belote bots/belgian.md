# Belgian Belote variants and bots

## What rules are documented at Belgian tables?

### Takeaway

The Belgian sources describe several actual rule profiles based on classic upcard Belote. Rules vary over taking after two rounds of passes, whether and how to undertrump, announcements and how to score the deal. The local variation affects both the engine and what the bot can infer.

### Cited Findings

- **Reumonjoie contest, Wallonia.** The organizer rules give a 101-point match on the reduced score scale with rounding upward starting at 6. An 81–81 `litige` banks the taking team's points for the next deal winner. The first bidding seat is forced after two rounds of passes, receives all eight before selecting any of the four suits, and a turned Jack doesn't force an immediate take. There are mandatory overtrumps and exceptions to the partner-master exemption for `pipi à 2` and `pipi à 3`. Declarations are spoken while playing on the first trick and shown on the second, and the same card cannot be counted in multiple melds. Four jacks score 200, nines 150, other allowed squares 100 and no sevens/eights. The King must be played before the Queen, both words announced, and the 20 points of Belote are inviolable. [Organizer rulebook](https://belote.rjcv.be/reglement2022.pdf)
- **Tilff 2026 competition, province of Liège.** Set format of four rounds of 12 deals, with raw points over the event. The forced player sees all eight before choosing a suit after two general passes. Melds are excluded except Belote, earned after announcing the King first even if the player omits “rebelote.” On 81–81 both sides keep 81. Capot is 252. The local rule requires an additional trump where a player cuts, an opponent also has to cut, and the first cutter's partner then plays. The organizer awards 182 to the deal winner if a Belote is present and the taking side goes down, and explicitly awards 252 plus 20 to the opposing holder when the capot winner did not hold Belote. [Action Culturelle Tilffoise rulebook](https://www.act-tilff.be/reglement-belote)
- **ABC Temploux tournament, Namur.** The rules explicitly prohibit a forced Jack and a forced third round, count melds except squares of 7/8, require the `pipi à deux/pipi à trois` trump rule, play even with fewer than 14 hand points and target 101. The short rules do not specify all score/tie details. [Organizer's tournament rules](https://www.basketclubs.be/abctemploux/news/tournoi-de-belotte-2019/)
- **Liège engineering student's rules, a firsthand campus example.** The 2025 Association des étudiants de l'École polytechnique de Liège magazine documents 5 cards plus an upcard, with a second round allowing the later seats to overcall a suit with sans atout or tout atout, and sans atout with tout atout. If everyone passes twice, the player left of the dealer gets the upcard and full eight-card hand before being forced to name a suit, sans atout or tout atout. A turned Jack forces that player to take its suit immediately. Someone with at most 14 hand points may request a redeal. The account describes mandatory undertrumping when a losing side has no higher trump, an exception to raising over a winning partner, King-first Belote, 81–81 carry, reduced score rounded upward at 6 and a 150-point target. Its no-trump scoring and Belote handling differ from Serrao. The magazine is evidence of a local profile and should not be treated as regulator or national rulebook. [L'Ingénu, pp. 56–59](https://aees.be/media/ingenus/pdfs/Ingenu_de_rentree_2025__Copy__compressed.pdf)
- **Belgian online implementation.** Belote-Rebelote.be identifies its 4-player profile as Belgian. It documents five cards plus upcard, take/pass then a different suit, redeal by next dealer after eight passes, trump/undertrump obligations, a partner-master exception, and optional announcements. The reduced score is 16 normal deal points with defense rounded and declarer gets the complement, 25 capot and 2 Belote; match to 101 with two-point margin. Its declared `litige` banks the taker's 81. [Operator game rules](https://www.belote-rebelote.be/regles-belote) · [Operator scoring rules](https://www.belote-rebelote.be/compter-points-belote)
- **Naming.** Couyon/couillon is a separate traditional game played in Belgium; the Sautour concours rulebook and the Royal Club Wallon source described by Pagat document different decks/calls/scoring from Belote. “Belote bridgée” is used for full-hand numerical-contract variants in French Belote literature; no primary Belgian source in the search established it as the default. The ULiège 2020 teaching brief gives 8-card hands and a supplied contract/trump, but explicitly omits bidding from the coursework and calls the model simplified. [Sautour Couyon rulebook](https://sautour.be/REGLEMENTS/Concours%20de%20COUYON%20REGLEMENT.pdf) · [Pagat's Couillon reference](https://www.pagat.com/couillon/couillon.html) · [ULiège Belote Referee brief](https://people.montefiore.uliege.be/info0004/files/oopp-project1-brief.pdf)

### Inferences

- A Belgian implementation can choose a named 101 table profile as the default and preserve separately configured options for the forced round timing, Jack override, all/no trump and overcalls, lower-point redeal, required undertrump, partner exception, meld rules and tie/match scoring. A raw-score fixed-deal tournament needs a different end condition and possibly a different objective from match-to-101.
- Choose table rules before designing card-consistency constraints. An off-suit discard reveals more under mandatory `pipi` than under optional undertrumping. A revealed meld pins particular hidden cards; the point carry in `litige` makes the current match score and bank relevant to action value.
- Live table practices on preserving deck order versus shuffling could change the deal distribution. Keep clearly recorded fresh shuffled evaluation deals and measure separately if reproducing a cut-only table. Campus and Reumonjoie sources both mention cutting.

### Gaps

- No national or Flemish federation rulebook specific to Belgian Belote found. Most detailed primary evidence is from francophone Belgian organizers. A Dutch-language generic rules site is not adequate evidence of a uniform Flemish profile.
- Some organizer rules assume club knowledge of `pipi` and standard dealing. Use a complete primary profile or concrete reference fixtures before treating exact legality/scoring as settled.

## What does existing bot work tell us about Belgian profiles?

### Takeaway

There is a Belgium-specific playable bot implementation. The public quantitative search benchmarks transfer as method evidence, while their absolute measured strength belongs to their own game models.

### Cited Findings

- The Belgian web app Belote-Rebelote.be explicitly offers bots to fill seats. Its developer identifies it as a Python/FastAPI/WebSockets project with a gameplay AI. The site gives rules and scoring, but neither it nor the portfolio publishes bot method, measured strength, or a repo link. [Belgian web app](https://www.belote-rebelote.be/) · [Developer's portfolio](https://www.kevinlefevre.dev/)
- Eryod Soft advertises a configurable AI for classic Belote with optional announcements, undertrumping, no/all trumps, forced Jack and partner calls. The vendor claims signalling, finesses, leading a singleton to ruff and returning partner's lead. This offers an additional playable comparison at some matching rules. Algorithm, ranked performance and a verified complete Belgian profile are unavailable. [Developer's app documentation](https://www.eryodsoft.com/fr/jeux/belote)
- **Pemeja** tests a French engine where players bid at 5 cards and see an upcard, closely matching the early phase of Belgian online/classic suit-only profiles. The simulator redeals after eight passes, uses raw single-deal score margin, omits general melds, and awards failed tie to defenders instead of carrying or splitting. The published Python source reveals automatic optional Belote/rebelote and no forced post-completion auction. Port the rollout approach and bidder architecture, then train/tune again on the Belgian rule profile. [Project](https://engineering.kaseovo.com/projects/belote-ai/) · [Python engine source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tpemeja/open_spiel/belote-cpp-rework/open_spiel/python/games/belote.py)
- **Serrao** models an upcard, five-card first auction, all/no trumps in round two and a forced first speaker after all pass. But the thesis's phase description places forced trump choice before the completion deal, unlike the after-eight Belgian examples, and no later-seat second-round overcall option is documented. Serrao's no-trump Ace value is 19 to reach 162, and his all-trump allows up to four Belotes with an unnormalized 258 base, unlike the Liège campus group's no-trump doubling and all-trump no-Belote rules. Its reported match win rates therefore apply to a related research simulator. [Serrao's thesis §§2.1, 4.2, 6](https://perso.uclouvain.be/eric.piette/master/2025%20-%20Performance%20Evaluation%20of%20Counterfactual%20Regret%20Minimization%20and%20Monte%20Carlo%20Tree%20Search%20Variants%20-%20A%20Case%20Study%20on%20Belote.pdf) · [Liège campus rules, pp. 56–59](https://aees.be/media/ingenus/pdfs/Ingenu_de_rentree_2025__Copy__compressed.pdf)

### Inferences

- Reuse the Bulgarian bot's deal sampling and card-play search code where the interfaces permit it. Apply Belgian rule fixtures to the legality, information-state inference and score modules. Rebuild bidding because five cards plus an upcard and the possible later eight-card forced decision produce different observations and legal actions from full hand or numerical contracts.
- Test the Belgian policy on the exact target match scoring. Pin bidding and public starting deal for card-play comparisons. For stronger tests add a named differently built partner/opponent and seat swaps on the same deals.

### Gaps

- No numerical strength benchmark against Belgian human players or a named Belgian club profile was found, and no source repo for the Belgian web bot was verified. The two academic institutions in Belgium establish where the work was done, not necessarily which local rules the engine implements.
